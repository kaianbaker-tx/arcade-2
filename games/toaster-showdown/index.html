Beat all 4 Toaster Bots!

Each bot is plugged in and full of electricity.

1. Yank the plug. Tap the plug on the wall 3 times to pull it out. 2. Poke the bot! Once it's unplugged, poke it fast before it plugs itself back in. 3. Block the toast. Burnt toast flies at you. Poke it before it hits the screen. ⚡ Don't poke a bot that's plugged in, or you get zapped!

Real-life rule: never put a fork in a toaster, even an unplugged one!
