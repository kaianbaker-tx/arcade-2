PIXEL GRIDIRON

8-bit football. Score more touchdowns than the other team before the clock hits 0:00.

P1 HOME
W A S D move · SPACE snap, throw, dive
P2 AWAY
ARROWS move · ENTER snap, throw, dive
OFFENSE
Press action to snap. Press it again to throw to your receiver before you cross the blue line. Or just run for it!
DEFENSE
You control the linebacker. Press action to DIVE and make the tackle.
DOWNS
You get 4 tries to reach the yellow line. Make it and you get 4 more.
