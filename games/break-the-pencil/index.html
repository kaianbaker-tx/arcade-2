Break the Pencil

Get through your to-do list while a mean pencil draws bananas, bullies and worse into your day. Make it to bedtime, then jump up and snap that pencil.

  • ← → walk
  • ↑ or Space jump
  • Click to throw an eraser there
  • F throw at the pencil

Every part of the day has three tasks. Walk to a task and stand on its dashed spot until it checks off (+mood). Miss a task and your mood drops. Erasers rub out anything the pencil drew, and every hit on the pencil weakens it for bedtime. Jump on a bully's head to bop him. At bedtime, press Space to jump up to the pencil, then mash Space to break it and go to sleep. If your mood hits zero first, the pencil wins.
