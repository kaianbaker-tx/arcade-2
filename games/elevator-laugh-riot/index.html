Elevator Laugh Riot

Get in the elevator, then keep everybody laughing all the way to the roof (floor 20). Every floor you get a few seconds to do something funny. Each rider secretly loves one thing and hates another. Bore them, gross them out, or do the same bit twice and they'll push you right out the door.

Or hold the right arrow key. Hurry, the doors are closing!

Keys 1–8 work too. Watch the laugh meters under each rider.
