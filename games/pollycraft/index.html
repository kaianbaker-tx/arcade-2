How to play Move WASD · Jump Space · Sneak Shift · Sprint double-tap W
Look move the mouse (click the world first) · Pause Esc
Mine hold the mouse button · Place / Use quick click (or right click) · Pick block middle click
Bag & crafting E · Hotbar 1–9 or scroll · Drop Q · Camera V or F5
Polly flies! Jump, then press Space again in the air to flap. Out of wing power? Hold Space to glide.
Squawk F scares nearby cats away. In Creative, double-tap Space to fly.
Start: punch a tree for logs, open your bag, craft planks, sticks, a crafting table, then a wooden pickaxe.
Your worlds save in this browser.
